# The desk's agent

(Until 2026-10-09 the UI called a space a "tab group", and a Profile a "Space"; identifiers keep the old words.)

A space is a task, shown as its desk (`docs/spaces.md`), and the agent can work at it with the person. The desk is the agent's context and its bounds: it sees every window and the space's tabs not out, keeps the task's own files and facts, and works in the space's tabs, where the person can watch it. Concepts and the choice between them: the "Desk Companion" study (claude.ai artifact, 30 Sep 2026). Chosen: the **Co-worker**, in the **Bar**, with the context as a **Stack**, the last row under the space's tabs in the sidebar (it was in the desk's own dock until the sidebar became the dock, 1 Oct 2026); the agent may rearrange windows; one conversation per space, any conversation can be continued in a space; the space's context syncs.

Desktop only (the desk is). Builds on `docs/desk.md` (the desk itself), `docs/console-routing.md` (turns) and `docs/notes.md` (the sync pattern the context follows).

## 1. What a person does

**The Bar** (2026-10-08, the "Droplet": `DeskBar.tsx`, `DeskNub.tsx`, `nub-motion.ts`). A nub in the desk's trailing foot corner: a droplet of the shell's own ground — a 36px circle partly sunk into the corner (its centre 20px in from both edges), joined to the foot and the trailing edge by 11px fillets, as if about to fall from the corner. It is not painted: it is a hole through the well and the windows under it, so it is the ground whatever the theme (docs/desk.md, *The foot*); over a live page main's notch view draws it. Its face is Pistachio's own mark, the nut of the brand mark (`PistachioGlyph`), in the space's colour (the agent's sparkle until 2026-10-08); while the agent works a thin arc of that colour runs round it, and while the answer floats a green dot sits on it. The pointer resting on it (80ms, so one passing over does not) swells it, the circle growing and drawn out of the corner as if pulled from it — 22px round, its centre 27px in, standing clear of both edges — while its fillets tighten (11 → 6px), so where it meets the edges reaches no further along them (the user's call, 2026-10-08: the circle grows, not the joins; it was 21px, 23px in, its fillets unchanged and its footprint along the edges widening) (the plus → menu morph's open, 350ms with a little overshoot; back 250ms, calm), and no page gives way for that (over a live page main's notch view draws the swell, read off the same clock as the shell's); resting there, its tooltip names the space and ⌘I — over a live page too, the view's hover opening it as the shell's nub's own does (`viewHover`), the band it appears in a cover like every Bar tooltip's, so the page under it is a still while it is up — the notch view staying over it, the pointer on it (docs/desk.md, *The notch view*). A click (or Enter) lets out its **menu** (2026-10-08, the user's call: the menu's close a button of its own, its droplets as round as it and fanned out rather than in a column): the nub fills with the card's fill (its outline painted over its hole) and lets go of the desk's edges — what is of it in the corner, its hole and the fill over it, drains into the corner as it lifts out, pulling a neck of goo from there that snaps, and it stands free, a 40px circle 8px clear of the foot and the trailing edge (`NUB_FREE`, `nubLetGo`), its mark the menu's close — and three droplets as round as it fan out of it over the desk's quarter, 70px from its centre (`NUB_FAN_R`): the prompt along the foot (180°), the microphone between (135°), past chats up the trailing edge (90°), each on a spring with a small overshoot, swept round from the foot up (28ms apart), stretched with its speed and pulling a neck of goo from the nub that thins and snaps; closing runs them back into it, the last out first back, as it settles into the corner again, and the fill goes once it is in. The goo is the public technique: the shapes blurred (5px) and their alpha thresholded (`feColorMatrix … 0 0 0 20 -8.5`) in one SVG, the necks lines drawn from the nub to the droplets, the buttons crisp above it; the whole layer is clipped to the desk's corner so neither the fill nor its shadow reaches the gutter. Each droplet has a tooltip to its left (the prompt's with ⌘I); the arrows move between them, Escape and a press anywhere else (a live page's too) put them back, the nub's own click too; the nub's mark turns into a close while they are out. A capture slows all of it (`--desk-nub-time-scale`). **Over a live page the menu is the notch view's too** (2026-11-17; `NotchApp`, `DeskNotchFrame.menu`): the page under it stays live and its own — a press on it is the page's, and puts the menu away as a press elsewhere always has. Until then the menu was a cover wherever it opened — the nub's box and the pill's place along the foot with it, so every page that reached the foot — and the page under it was its still for as long as the droplets were out: a press on that picture was a press on the window, and dragged it carried the window off (one filling the desk let go of it). The view draws the shell's own nub again (`DeskNub`, with a `NubMotion` of its own, told when the menu went out or in), in a box the shell grows to the droplets' fan while they are out (`NUB_BOX`, held while they run back; the engine's `setNotch` takes the same box, so a live page anywhere under the fan counts as under the nub); a droplet pressed there is the shell's pressed (`DeskNotchInput`'s `{ menu }`, main handing the shell the keyboard), the pointer resting on one opens the shell's tooltip beside it (`{ hover }`, the tooltip's band a cover the view lies over, as the nub's own), and a press in the view's box beside the droplets is a press elsewhere (`away`). The shell's own menu stands veiled under the view's meanwhile (`data-veiled`: unseen, in place for its tooltips). With the window's glass on, what of the nub is still in the corner as it lets go stays the glass's (the nub's picture follows the same motion, and its outline with it). Two menus are covers still (`menuPath`): one opened from the keys — the keyboard is among the shell's droplets, and the ring round the one that has it is the shell's to draw — and one opened while extensions' pills stand, which leave the nub's lane for the pill's place. What the menu opens is a cover as before: the conversations, and the pill's place for the moment its page takes to give way, the droplet it grows out of waiting there for it (`morphing`).

The prompt's droplet becomes the **pill**: it detaches and morphs (its glass from the droplet's box to the pill's, a little past and back; its row fading and blurring in as it is uncovered) into a floating glass field at the desk's foot, 52px tall — the Bar's own height before the nub, its row's padding, buttons (30px, send 32px) and field as they were then (the user's call, 2026-10-08, after a day at 40px) — and up to 480px wide, its words 15px, right-aligned 54px in from the trailing edge so the nub stands beside it — tied to the nub by a thin bridge of goo for the first half of the morph, then let go (the goo layer lies under the pill's glass, so only the bridge shows). It holds the message field (its placeholder names the space), a tray (attach, a new conversation), the answer chevron, the microphone and send (stop while the agent works; the field then steers), each button with a tooltip (drawn under a cover, `bar-tip` — the band above the row where the row is: in the floating card, where the card is drawn, not where it is laid out, 2026-10-10; the @ list's cover there follows the card as it moves, and goes with the list between the pill and the card). In a floating card the field grows only so far (two fifths of the card) and the files staged take only so many rows (a fifth, scrolling past that), so the row stays whole in a card at its least height (2026-10-10). It stays out while anything holds it — something typed, staged or warned of, dictation, the agent at work, the answer docked on it, the @ list, files on their way, the keyboard in it — and goes back into the nub (shrinking into its face, calm) as soon as none does; Escape with nothing in it puts it back. While it is out, its **close** stands above the nub (2026-10-08, the user's call): a small round button, 32px, 6px in from the trailing edge where the menu's droplets once stood in a column (`nubClose`), the card's fill and a droplet's shadow, coming up out of the nub as the pill comes out and going back into it (not while the menu is out, where it would stand among the droplets). It puts the pill away whatever holds it — what was typed and staged is kept for when it comes out again, a recording is dropped, and the answer docked on it closes (a floating one stays) — which a press elsewhere does not do to a pill that holds something. Put away so (`stowed`), the pill stays away until something asks for it again: the keyboard coming to its field (⌘I, a droplet), files dragged to it, an answer docking on it. The pill and the nub's menu are one or the other, never both (the user's call, 2026-10-08: the menu's droplets fanned across the pill's end): the nub pressed with the pill out puts the pill away as its close does — what is in it kept, the answer docked on it closed — and lets out the menu; whatever asks for the pill puts the menu back in. ⌘I puts the keyboard in its field (on the desktop, ⌘I is the Bar's, not the sidebar chat's — also from inside a page, which main relays), the pill then growing out of the nub itself. Its box is always laid out where it rests (the answer docks above it, a floating one keeps clear of it). Everything out of the nub is a cover (`bar`, the nub's box with its fan; `pill`; `conversations`), drawn once the pages under it have given way (or after 300ms), and a cover until it is back in (280ms) — but the pill's cover holds nothing for long (2026-11-05): out, the desk keeps a band clear at its foot for it (`DeskEngine.setBand`, up to the pill's footprint: its box and the gap above it) and the windows glide up out of it, their pages live, as they give way to the chat's place at the side; the pill lies on the ground below them, and they come back down as it goes in. (Until then a page that reached the foot was its still, dead to the pointer, for as long as the pill was out.) **Since 2026-11-18, on the desktop, the pill lies on the pages and no window moves for it** (the user's call: "the browser window shouldn't move up or change size to accommodate the text input. The row (`composerBox`) lies in the view's document while the pill is out, the Bar's same React tree; the shell's own pill keeps its box under it, unseen, at the view's pill's height, so the answer docked on it stands where it did (a cover still); the @ and `/` lists stand on the view's pill, in the view, and are no cover. Extensions' live pills at the foot, which the pill once took into its cover, stand beside it in the view (the view's box reaches left over them). The band and the pill's cover remain only where the view cannot be had: on the web. In the view the pill's buttons have no tooltips (their band is the shell's, under the page), and the pill comes in with a short fade and slide of its own rather than morphing out of the droplet, which is drawn by another view. Its close and the side button above the nub stand in the column those windows reach into, so over a live page main's notch view draws them, as it draws the nub (docs/desk.md, *The notch view*; `DeskNotchFrame.buttons`), the shell's own veiled in place under them. Escape unwinds one layer at a time: the conversations, the menu, the answer docked, the pill if empty. Until 2026-10-08 the Bar was a notch centred on the desk's foot that grew into a 52px field there; until 2026-10-02 a glass pill floating a gap above the desk's foot, the desk keeping a band for it (`BAR_BAND`).

**Dictation.** The nub's microphone droplet opens the pill already listening, and the pill's microphone button listens: the field gives way to a waveform of what it hears, scrolling in from the trailing end, and a clock, with discard (×) at the leading end and Done (✓) where send was. Done, or Enter, transcribes what was said with the host's speech-to-text (main's `transcribeSpeech`, the walkthrough's) and puts it in the field where the caret was, spaced from the words either side, to be read back and sent; it is never sent by itself. Escape or × drops the recording, even one being transcribed. A tap under 0.8 s is not sent; a recording stops itself at two minutes and is transcribed. A refused microphone, a failed transcription or silence says so in the Bar's banner, and the typed text stays. The logic is `lib/dictation.ts` (`Dictation`, free of React, and `useDictation`); the waveform is `components/desk/DictationWave.tsx`.

**Commands** (2026-10-16; `lib/desk/slash-commands.ts`, the catalog and everything about it that is not drawing; `DeskBar.tsx`'s `CommandMenu`, `chooseCommand`, `runCommand`). A `/` at the head of the field lets out the commands over it, on the @ list's glass and in its place (the two are one menu over the field, `FieldMenuState`, and one cover): bare, every command under its group — *Browse* (`/search`, `/open`, `/find`), *Ask Pistachio* (`/summarize`, `/compare`, `/explain`, `/translate`, `/research`, `/new`, and — to Pistachio's makers, since 2026-11-16 — `/feedback`), *Desk* (`/arrange`, `/tidy`, `/remember`, `/screenshot`, `/share`, `/draw`), *Open* (`/note`, `/brief`, `/terminal`), *Extensions* (each extension's declared commands, and each Chrome extension on the Ledge as its action — `ext:` ids, `barCommandsFor`; main runs them: docs/extensions.md §2), *Make* (`/extension`: a draft folder, then the wish to Pistachio, §8) — its foot fading while more is below; typed, narrowed to those whose name starts so, then (from two letters) those another name of theirs does (`/google`, `/tldr`), then those each typed word begins a word of — never letters scattered through a row. Each row is its mark (the search engine's own for `/search`), `/name` and what it does. The arrows walk it round, Enter runs the lit one with whatever follows its word — or, for one that must have something (`/search`, `/open`, `/find`, `/translate`, `/research`, `/remember`), puts it in the field — Tab always puts it in the field, a click runs it, and Escape puts the menu away until the field's `/` goes. A command in the field is marked as a mention is, and while nothing follows it what it takes is hinted after it, in the field's type ("what to search for"); Enter with nothing there yet does nothing (Send is unavailable). Sent, a message whose first word is a command's name is that command (any other `/word` is a message): a request to Pistachio goes as the message it stands for, worded as a person would ask it (`commandPrompt`: "Summarize the page in the window I'm using.") so the conversation reads, and is named, as asked; the rest the Bar does itself and the field empties (files staged stay) — `/search` a search on the person's engine (Settings → General → Search) and `/open` an address (a search when it is none) in a new tab of the space, as the address bar's ⌥↵ does; `/find` smart find on the window in use (its Find bar shows the words, open already or not: `FindCommand.fill`, docs/smart-find.md §5); `/new` a new conversation (refused while the agent works); bare `/arrange` the desk's own layout (⌘⌥L), with words a request to Pistachio; `/tidy` Tab Tidy; `/remember` a fact saved to the space's context, as the Stack's field does; `/screenshot` ⌘⇧1; `/share` the space live, or its Live card when it is shared already, and `/draw` the host's pen over the shared desk, on or off (docs/live-share.md §1); `/note` a new note, titled with what follows; `/brief` and `/terminal` as their shortcuts. The placeholder says "/ for commands".

**The answer.** A card of the same glass, resting on the pill, as wide as it (they share one column; 2026-10-08, the user's call — it was 440px, a little narrower), however tall the pill grows, opening out of the pill's trailing end (its clip grows from a circle there, the plus → menu morph's open, played once as it comes: a clip left on it would make it a backdrop root, its glass blurring nothing), shows the latest exchange: what was asked, the agent's steps as it takes them, its reply as it streams, and anything it asks (a question, an approval, a takeover). The agent's side of a turn is drawn as **trace lines** (2026-10-16: shadcn/ui's Trace and Code Block, ported to `components/ui/trace.tsx` and `code-block.tsx` on Base UI's Collapsible; `components/chat/trace.tsx`, `lib/chat-trace.ts`; AI Elements' Reasoning, Chain of Thought and Stack Trace patterns). What the model thought is a line — "Thinking", a clock beside it, open on the latest few lines of it, faded at the top; then "Thought" and how long, shut, opening to the whole of it. A turn's steps fold under one line: the step in hand, shimmering, while the turn works (through the model's thinking between two steps too), then what the turn was and how many steps it took, and how many failed; each step is a line with its tool's mark, opening to what it said and the page it read (a Glance, as the reply's links), and one that failed is red, says so ("Failed: Read page"), and opens to its error, whole, in a monospace box with a copy button. A run that stopped on an error ends on its reply's sentence as a red line ("I stopped because … failed"), open on the error, the reply's actions under it — the reply read off `#failRun`'s wording and the run's last blocked activity, so any other reply draws as before. Each line opens while its work goes on and shuts when it is done, unless the person opened or shut it. The sidebar console and the home chat keep their steps list and reasoning ticker (`chat/parts.tsx`). It opens when a turn starts (the person's message, or the agent asking something) and stays until collapsed (its chevron, or Escape); the Bar's chevron brings it back. "Whole conversation" in its header shows every turn, scrolled to the end. While the card is up, the windows under it are stills (it is a cover). No "I'm on it" intro and no "browsing in another tab" chip at a desk: the agent is seen on the windows.

**The agent at the desk.**
- *Where it is*: the window whose tab it is working in (`agentDrivenTabId`) wears the agent's comet ring on its frame and a chip saying what it is doing (Reading, Clicking, Typing, Opening, Arranging, Noting, Thinking between steps) — in the bar frame's title row, or above a tab or handle frame. The tab's row in the sidebar wears the ring too (on its icon). The page itself glows while the agent drives it (the glow main already injects).
- *Windows it opens* join the space and come out onto the desk beside the window in use, under it in the stack, never taking the keyboard or the selection.
- *Arranging*: it may place, tile, cascade, put away and bring out windows. After a turn that moved windows, the answer card offers **Undo layout**, which puts every window back where it was before the turn.
- *Notes on windows*: it may pin a short note (80 characters) to a window's frame ("Lands 11:05, before check-in"), beside the chip. Notes stay until the next turn starts or the person dismisses them (×). A note needs the window out on the desk.
- *Out of the space*: moving a tab out of the space is asked first.

**Conversations.** Each space has its own. The desk's conversation is the current space's (or a new, empty one), and passing the desk to another space opens that space's; main follows the current space itself (`runs.followGroup`). (Until 2026-10-09 opening the desk opened the space's conversation, and leaving it went back to the conversation that was open before.) The nub's past-chats droplet itself grows into the list (the plus → menu morph: its glass grows up and left from where the droplet rests, its corners relaxing from the droplet's round into 14px, its mark cross-fading out as the rows slide in; back into the droplet quicker and calm, or fading as it shrinks when the menu goes too; while it is out the other droplets run back into the nub, out of its way, and fan out again when it goes back with the menu still out), 320px wide with thin rows (30px); it lists every conversation, each marked with where it started (This space, another space by name, Another space when that space is not here, Sidebar, Reminder): choosing one continues it here, and from then on it is this space's (the space it came from keeps it too). New conversation, the list's first row, starts an empty one for the space. A press anywhere else (a live page's too, which main relays) puts the list and the menu away; Escape puts the list back into its droplet, and a second the menu into the nub; choosing a conversation puts both away. While the agent is acting in this space's conversation, nothing else can be chosen here (a turn at work on this Mac: a cloud run holds nothing — it goes on in the cloud browser, and the space may take up another conversation or a new one meanwhile, `agentHoldsChat`). (Until 2026-11-16 a turn still running went with the person from space to space, and kept the Bar wherever they were: §3, "Conversations stay in their space".) A conversation at work in another space — or a reminder's task, which is no space's — can be chosen like any other: it is shown here too, live, the same conversation.

**The chat is its space's** (2026-11-16; `lib/desk/space-chat.ts`, `answer-float.ts`'s spots, `DeskBar.tsx`). The Bar is one, drawn for whichever space is in front, and everything of the chat belongs to that space, not to the window:

- *Where it stands.* Docked on the pill, floating, snapped to a tile or in the chat's place at the side — each space's own (and Home's, for Home: its tabs are each a space of their own under the hood, with a conversation each, but Home is one place to the person — the chat taken to the side beside one of its tabs comes up at the side beside any other; *whether* it is up is each tab's own, below), kept on this device across launches (`pistachio.desk.answer.v2`: a place per space id, the 200 most recently kept; the one place there was before, `…answer.v1`, goes to the first space that asks and to no other). The chat taken to the side in one space is at the side *there*; the next space the person goes to shows its own chat where it was left — in its nub, if it was never taken out — and its windows have its whole desk (the room kept for the chat's place is the leaving space's, and goes with it; back there, the windows give way again and stand exactly where they stood).
- *What is in it.* Whether the card was up, the whole conversation or the last turn, the words in the message field and the files staged: put away as the desk passes on, and taken out again as they were (for this run of the app; the composer is made anew for each space, `key={group.id}`).
- *Whether it is up is the person's word, and never the desk's coming* (2026-11-17; `chatUpOnArrival`). A space's chat is up as the desk comes to it only if it was up when the person left — and one they closed stays closed, at the side as anywhere. So a space of windows keeps its chat beside them for as long as the person has it up, there and back, and the next time Pistachio opens (not left yet this run, a chat that stood at its own space's side is up there). A tab of Home's is a space of its own under the hood: the chat up beside one tab is not up beside the next, a change of tab opens nothing, and closed beside a tab it is closed there when the person is back. Its × at the side is kept (`FloatSpot.shut`), so the next launch does not bring it up either; asked for again — the pill's answer button, the side button, a message sent — it comes up where it stands. Until then a chat in the chat's place was up whenever a space of that place was in front: its × lasted only until the space was left, and, Home being one place for every tab, a chat at Home's side opened again with every change of tab.
- *Whose conversation.* Only this space's: `selectSpaceRun` gives the desk the snapshot's run once main's word is for the space in front (`ShellSnapshot.runSpace`), so for the moment after a change of space — the tabs and the run travel on separate channels — the Bar shows no conversation rather than the last space's.
- *Coming back.* A space's chat is as it was left, unless the agent went on meanwhile (§3): more said since, or something newly asked of the person, and its card comes up to show it (`newSinceLeft`). What already stood asked when they left, the card put away, is not asked again by every return: the space's tile on the rail says it waits.
- *The activity record is a conversation's.* Asked for in one space and answered once the person is in another, it is dropped rather than shown over the conversation in view (`loadEvidence`).
- *Across a change of Profile.* The desk, and the Bar with it, are mounted anew for each Profile (`ContentArea`'s `DeskMount`) — no passing of the desk, so the Bar keeps what its space's chat holds as it goes (`keepOnLeave`) and starts from it when it next comes up: the card, the words, and the files staged (`useAttachmentDrop`'s `initial`) — and, as on any return to a space, the card comes up if more was said there meanwhile.
- *What a page adds is the space's it was added in.* "Add … to Chat" names the space in front as the item is chosen (the `attachToChat` command's `groupId`; main reads a picture before it sends it): one that arrives once the person is in another space is staged for the first, with what it was left with (`chatInsertElsewhere`, `stageInSpace`), and a picture that could not be read is said in a notice rather than in another space's field.
- *A file still being read is its own space's.* A file dropped or chosen in one space and read once the desk has passed on is staged for the space it was dropped in — with what that space was left with — never added to the list on screen, which is another's by then (`attachmentSink`, `useAttachmentDrop`'s `owner`) — or, when a Bar is up for that space by then (the person back from another Profile, the desk made anew), taken into the list on screen (`takeStagedFor`). A Word or Excel file taken into the space's context mentions itself in that space's field: the one on screen while that space is in front, else the words it was left with.
- *What a card writes as it goes is its own space's.* The card's motion keeps where it stands for the place it was made for (`motionOwner`; made anew whenever the desk passes to a space of another place than the one it keeps for — counted, `motionKey`, since a Home tab that broke away takes the motion with it and the next Home tab is Home's place again): one still on its way back to the pill as the desk passes to a space of another place writes "docked" for the space it is leaving, never over the place of the one arrived at.
- *Asked about a tab of another space* (its menu's Ask Pistachio, the shell's `delegate`): the tab is chosen first, and the Bar asked once the desk has passed to its space — asked at once, the field of the space being left would take the keyboard and go with it.
- *Leaving.* The space's card and pill go as the desk passes, in the very commit the space changes — before the desk's engine has passed (its effect runs after the Bar's). The window they covered is on its way out, and is not handed the keyboard it was waiting for (`DeskEngine.leaving`): main would make its tab the one in use again, over the tab just chosen in the space the desk is passing to, and the person would be put straight back where they came from.

And it is **the desktop's only chat** (2026-11-16): the sidebar's console — the panel at the window's trailing side — is no longer drawn where there is a desk (`SidebarLayout`; it is the web's chat still, which has no desk). What used to open it brings the space in front's chat out of its nub instead — a reminder firing (Settings → Reminders, *Open the agent chat*), a press on a notification about a conversation, the agent pausing for an approval (*Open the chat on a pause*), the brief's *ask*, *Open the agent chat on launch*: the store's `consoleOpen`, which `DeskSurface` answers once (`chatSignal`) — the answer card up and in front. With nothing to show yet, what it was wanted for is shown as it comes, within a few seconds (the conversation main's word of is a moment behind; a reminder's card not yet recorded), and the keyboard is never taken for it — a reminder fires while the person is typing in a page. Only as the app starts does the pill come out with the keyboard in it. A conversation that begins in this space by another hand than the Bar's (the brief's ask) brings its card up too. What the console alone had is the card's and the pill's now:

- *Add … to Chat* from a page's right-click menu stages the picture or the selection in the Bar's field, the pill out for it (`chat/chat-inbox.ts`, shared with the console).
- The conversation's **activity record** (⌘E, the card's header button, the reply's *View n activity records*) is drawn in the card, in the conversation's place, until closed (`chat/EvidenceReplay.tsx`); another conversation in the card puts it away.
- **Reminders that fired** and wait to be seen are cards at the head of the card's conversation — and a reason for the card to come up with no conversation at all (`ReminderInbox`). A conversation a reminder started says so at its head (*Scheduled · …*), and the task's notes stand there too (`TaskNotes`), when the card shows the conversation from its head (the whole conversation, the chat's place).
- The **context meter** rides the line of the reply being written.
- In the chat's place, where the header has a column's room (400px and up; the header keeps a grip beside the name): the **feedback** form. From anywhere, `/feedback` and the words in the Bar sends the same report (the conversation attached, no reaction), and says it was received. A cloud run's **live view** button is in the header wherever the card stands.
- The pill's tray has **Run in cloud** beside attach and a new conversation: the next conversation runs in the cloud browser rather than at this desk (a fresh conversation's choice; a refusal is said in the Bar's banner, the words kept). The files it @mentions go with it as they do at the desk — their text, or the file — and one that cannot is named as left behind, since the cloud has none of the space's context to read it from. Where it runs is read as it is sent — the Profile and the page in view (`cloudStartContext`), named in the request — not whichever are in view once those files have been read. How the start went is the asking space's (`lib/desk/cloud-start.ts`): control takes its time, and with the person in another space by then, a start that was taken empties the field that space was left with, and a refusal keeps it and is said in a notice — the space they went to keeps its own field and files. One start at a time from a space. The conversation control makes is the asking space's from then on: the request names the space (`CloudStartRunRequest.groupId`), and main binds the run to it as it is made (`RunHub.cloudStarted`) and shows it there as its first event is folded into the store — or when the person is back, if they have gone on — so its answer comes up in this space's card and the next message here continues it. A start control has taken stays under way until that conversation is in the space (`cloudStartTaken`, up to `CLOUD_ARRIVAL_MS`): a message sent in the moment between would start a second cloud conversation instead of continuing the first, so nothing more is sent from the space while a start is under way — to the cloud or to this Mac, where the same words would run twice — and the tray's cloud button waits too (`cloudStarting`, `watchCloudStarts`): the field keeps the words and says the conversation is starting. `/extension` is always this Mac's, whatever the button says: the draft is a folder here, written by main's author turn. A start whose conversation never comes is given up on after that long, and the next one takes its place as the space's (main lets a second start replace a binding to a run there is still nothing of). (Until 2026-11-17 it was only in the past chats, as it was in the console's list.)
- A conversation that is paused with nothing asked of the person — stopped by them, by the turn's own allowance, by a restart — has **Resume** at its foot (`desk-answer-resume`; main's `releaseControl`): the turn picks up from its last finished step. (A takeover the agent asked for has its own card.) - The past chats delete a conversation: its row's ×, pressed twice (the first press arms it). A conversation at work — here or in the space it is at home in — says *Working* (or *Needs you*) where its time was, and cannot be deleted.

Not at a desk: the console's page-in-view chip (docs/console-routing.md §5.1) — at a desk the space is the subject, and the Bar's message names no page — and its welcome (prompt suggestions, recent threads; the home page is that).

## 2. What the agent gets

**Scope.** A turn in the desk's conversation runs on a scoped browser: `tabs_list` lists only the space's tabs and the agent's own hidden ones, and every page tool refuses any other tab with an error that says to ask the person and open its address with `tab_open`. `tab_open` opens a hidden tab, as everywhere on the desktop (`BrowserController.openHiddenTab`): out of the space, off the desk, out of the person's sight, so the agent looks things up while the desk stays as the person has it. A page the agent's own click or key opens is hidden too (`holdPopupsHidden`). Only `tab_show` puts a hidden tab on the desk — when the person asks to open or see a page, or to put it on the desk: it joins the space (`showHiddenTabInGroup`) and comes out quietly, beside the window in use. The scope is the controller's, not the prompt's: `DeskScope` wraps `DesktopBrowserBackend`. (A page's own `window.open` or `target="_blank"` from one of the desk's tabs on the person's click opens beside it in its space, before it is shown: `BrowserController`'s window-open handler, `#onDesk`, `createTab({ beside })`. A desk turn holds its space for as long as it runs (`holdGroup`), so this goes on after the person passes the desk to another space mid-turn.)

**The desk block.** Each turn's message carries the desk as it stands, read from the shell at the turn's start: the space's name, every window (tab id, title, address, box as percent of the desk, stacking, which is in use), the space's tabs not out, and the context's items (id, kind, name or text). A few hundred tokens; pages are read with the page tools as ever.

**Desk tools** (tool group `desk`, host-supplied, only on a desk turn):

| Tool | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `desk_state` | The desk block, fresh. |
| `desk_arrange` | Tile or cascade every window; place windows in a zone (halves, quarters, center, full) or a box; put windows away into the sidebar; bring the space's other tabs out. Focus and the keyboard never move. |
| `desk_note` | Pin a short note to a window's frame, or clear it. |
| `desk_ungroup` | Move tabs out of the space (they stay open). Asked of the person first. |
| `context_read` | Read a context item: a fact's text, a text file's text, an image or PDF as a file the model sees. |
| `context_save` | Save a fact or snippet to the space's context. |

Desk turns always take the browser path (no answer-path routing, route basis `skipped`): the desk tools and pages are the point of a desk. `DESK_RULES` join the browse prompt: work from the desk block, stay on the desk, arrange confidently (Undo exists) and say so, note sparingly, read and save context, ask before ungrouping, name windows by title.

## 3. How it works

**Conversations stay in their space** (2026-11-16; `run-hub.ts`, `RunHub`). A *console* (`RunController`) holds one conversation: its thread, its model history, the turn it is running. There was one console, and it followed the person — a turn still running in the space they left went with them to the next, and kept its Bar there. Now the hub keeps a console for every conversation that has something going, and shows the one of the space in front:

- *The space in front shows its own.* Main's current space changing (`BrowserControllerHooks.onCurrentGroupChange` → `runs.followGroup`, `docs/spaces.md` §2) brings up the console holding the conversation bound to that space (`desk-conversations.json`, space → thread, `DeskConversationStore`), or opens it from the store, or an empty console where the space has none yet. Everything the shell asks — a message, an approval, Stop, `choose` and `new` (`deskConversation`, each checked against `currentGroupId()`) — is asked of that console. The run publish says which space it is for (`runSpace`).
- *A message is the space's whose Bar sent it.* The Bar names its space with the message (`AgentTurnOptions.groupId`), and main runs it in that space's conversation (`RunHub.start`, `message`): the console of the space in front, as a rule. One still on its way as the person left — a file it mentions was being read — is its own space's all the same: it runs there, behind them, in the console that space's conversation is in or one opened for it, as a turn at the sending space's desk whichever space that console is at home in (a conversation may be two spaces', and shown in the other by then: `RunController`'s `#deskTurn` takes the turn's own space) (a conversation it starts names no tab of the space in view, and is its space's Profile's), and the space they went to is handed nothing. A message for a space that is gone is refused. A space's messages go in the order they were sent, whichever composer sent each (`queueSpaceSend`: the Bar's composer is made anew when the desk passes away and back, and a later message must not overtake one still reading its file). The line holds a message only while its files are read: it is handed to main, never waited on — main answers when the turn it started has ended, and a message sent while the agent works is a steer of that turn, which reaches it at once. (2026-11-17; until then every message was the space in front's as main heard it.)
- *One with something going stays where it is.* The console the person left is kept, and goes on, while it is *engaged*: a turn acting, or a question, an approval or a takeover they have yet to answer. Nothing is cancelled for their having looked elsewhere, nothing goes with them, and it is in its console still — not read back from the store — when they return. An idle one is saved and set aside, as leaving a conversation always did (its research tabs closed), and its console is reused for the next space: with no agent at work there is one console, as there always was.
- *Each goes at its own pace.* Two spaces' turns run side by side, each in its own tabs (a desk turn is scoped to its space's: `DeskScope`, `holdGroup`). The shell draws one desk, so the desk tools of a turn whose space is not in front are refused — "the person is looking at another space now…" — and its turn goes on with the rest; a turn that starts so (an answer from afar) is told in its prompt's desk block.
- *The tiles say what goes on.* `ShellSnapshot.spaceAgents` (`RunHub.spaceAgents`): by space, `working` (a turn runs there), `asking` (it waits on the person) or `answered` (a turn ended there that they have not looked at since — until that space next comes to the front, and only for as long as that conversation is still the space's: deleted from another space's past chats, or the space given another, its tile says nothing). The rail draws it on the tiles of the spaces the person is not in (`AgentMark`: a ring turning, an amber dot, a green one; Home's tile says the most pressing of its tabs' spaces), and says it in the tile's name. A background console's changes are published only when the list of conversations or these would say something else — not for every word of a reply nobody is reading.
- *A cloud conversation's tile is told from control.* The cloud browser goes on without this Mac, and a console here is let go of when the person leaves a cloud conversation's space (it has nothing of its own going), so what its space's tile says — `working`, `asking`, and `answered` once it stops with the space not in front — is read from each state control reports (`RunHub.refreshRemote`, `#remote`), whoever holds the conversation.
- *Chosen across Profiles.* A conversation at work in another Profile, chosen from the past chats, is shown in the space it was chosen in like any other: the snapshot's old rule — a run the agent is driving shows only in its tab's Profile — is for a conversation nobody placed, and gives way to the hub's word that this is the space in front's (`run-scope.ts`, `runInView`).
- *A reminder's task is no space's.* It runs in a console of its own, never in front by itself: it takes nobody's conversation from them, and no longer waits for theirs to end (they still run one at a time among themselves). It is in the past chats as it works (*Reminder*), and choosing it there — or pressing its notification (`RunHub.reveal`) — shows it in the space the person is in.
- *An answer from afar* (iMessage) runs where its question waits, in its own space, and nobody is moved. (Until 2026-11-16 the asking space was brought to the front for it, and an answer that came while another conversation was acting waited for that turn.)
- *Retry is the conversation's.* The last turn of each conversation, as it was started, is kept by the conversation (`TurnCheckpoints`, shared by the hub's consoles; in memory, the most recent 32) — not by the console that ran it, which is let go of when a turn ends behind the person and given other conversations as they move about. Back in the space, the reply's Retry runs that turn again; and a console never retries the conversation it holds from the checkpoint of one it held before. The thread an author turn is writing an extension in (docs/extensions.md §8) is kept the same way: paused, and resumed once its space's console has been let go of, it is an author turn still.
- *One conversation is in one console.* A space bound to a conversation at work in another space shows that same console. When one of its spaces lets go of it — a new conversation there, another chosen — while it has something going, it is left as it stands for the space that still has it (its question not cancelled, its research not closed), at home there, and the space that let go has a console of its own (`#leaveShared`). A message that begins a conversation of its own — the Bar's `/extension` — is handled the same way wherever it was sent from: it starts in a console at home in the sending space, bound to that space as it starts, and never in the console of a conversation shown in another. A press on a notification about a conversation goes to it (`reveal`): its space comes to the front.
- *Across a quit.* The conversation open at the quit comes back at launch (`restore`) at home in its own space, before main names the space in front: one that waits on the person still is kept behind another space, and an answer from afar then runs it as its space's turn — never loose in the whole browser, as a console with no home is. Every console is paused with the window (`shutdown`). A thread read from the store that says a turn was under way, or waits on an approval, was cut short — by a crash, since a console holds such a thread for as long as the app runs — and comes back paused whenever it is next opened (`RunController.open`; until 2026-11-16 only the thread open at the last quit was looked at).

A thread started in a space is marked with it (`RunSummary.groupId`, on `ThreadListItem` too) and bound to the space whose Bar it was. A turn is desk-scoped when its console's space (`home`: the one it was last shown in) is bound to its thread (never a cloud run). It works on an empty space too (no tab in use). (Until 2026-10-09 the shell said when a desk was up and for which space — `deskConversation` `enter`, again for each space it passed to — and when it was left, `leave`, which went back to the conversation open before; a shell reload left the desk, `did-start-loading`. `enter` and `leave` are kept as no-ops so an older shell's command is not refused.)

**Desk bridge** (`desk-bridge.ts`). Main asks the shell for what only the shell knows (the desk's layout) and to change it: a request with an id on `pistachio:desk-request`, the reply by `deskReply(id, …)`, with a 5 s timeout (a failed reply is an ordinary tool error). Every request names the space whose desk it is for, and the shell refuses one for a desk no longer in view before it changes anything (the person may pass the desk to another space mid-turn). The shell answers from the desk's engine (`desk-requests.ts`, fed by `DeskSurface`), trimming titles and addresses to the reply's bounds (`MAX_DESK_TITLE`, `MAX_DESK_URL`, `MAX_DESK_DOCKED`): `state` (`agentLayout`), `arrange` (`arrangeFor`, which refuses strangers, putting away the window in use, or a person's gesture in progress, and then moves nothing), `bringOut` (waits up to 2 s for the tab to reach the shell's space, then `bringOutQuietly` — under the window in use, no selection), `note`. Each reply is checked in main to be the same space's desk. Before the first change of a turn the shell keeps `layoutSnapshot()`; the answer card's **Undo layout** is `restoreLayout` for that turn. A window still on its way into the sidebar when it is wanted back (Undo, or the agent placing or bringing it out again) turns back (`#recall`, making room first on a full desk) rather than being lost to the dock; one on its way into the Close pad or another space's icon goes on — that was the person's doing.

**The Bar's notch** (`DeskEngine.setNotch`, `setNotchShape`). The idle notch's size, centred on the desk's foot, is what windows under it are cut short of; the notch as drawn now is the hole cut through the well and the windows — drawn out along the foot while extensions' live pills lie in it (its lane, 2026-10-30: docs/desk.md, *The foot*). The engine's `#usable` and `#reach` are the whole card, short of the room kept for the chat's place at its trailing side (`setReserve`) and, while the pill is out, of the pill's band at its foot (`setBand`, 2026-11-05; until 2026-10-02 they stopped above the Bar's band always, `setBarBand`). The agent's zones and boxes are percents of `#usable` (`lib/desk/agent.ts`: `deskZoneRect`, `percentBox`, `boxRect`).

**A space's context** (`group-context-store.ts`). One index file and content-addressed files in user data (`group-context.json`, `group-blobs/<sha24>`, owner-only permissions). Synced as two record kinds, sealed with the account's workspace key like notes: `group-context:<groupId>` (LWW: the space's title when written, its items, and the files' ids) and `group-blob:<sha24>` (a file's bytes, only files up to `MAX_GROUP_BLOB_BYTES`; bigger ones stay on the Mac they were dropped on and are listed elsewhere as not here). Spaces do not sync live between Macs; a space pulled from another Mac keeps its id, so its context is there with it.

## 5. Not done / open questions

- Masking a region of a page (`window_mask`), pointing at an element in a page, and closing tabs are not desk tools yet.
- Cloud runs have no desk: a cloud conversation opened in a space is not desk-scoped.
- The context's files are read by the model as they are (a PDF as a PDF); nothing is summarised when it is dropped.
- A context brought from another Mac is a copy: the two spaces' contexts go their own ways afterwards.
- The Stack's files show as type glyphs, not previews.
- A text or link dragged out of a live page onto the Stack is untested against real pages (the e2e drops a file synthetically).
- No live-model measurement yet of how well the agent uses the desk tools (the e2e and unit tests script the model).
- The cloud browser's record allow-list does not carry space contexts (by design: it has no desk).
- The windows make room for the chat's place in proportion (the whole arrangement scaled into what is left), not by moving only those it would cover; and it is on the trailing side only.
- Dictation is transcribed when the person is done, not word by word as they speak (the host's speech-to-text takes a whole recording). The sidebar console's composer has its own copy of the same recording logic; it could move onto `useDictation`.
- The console is still in the tree, for the web (`AgentConsole.tsx`, `PanelShell.tsx`, the thread list): the Bar is the desk's, and the web has no desk. Until the pill and the card are lifted out of the desk for it, the panel's code stays; the desktop draws none of it.
- The feedback form is in the card's header only in the chat's place (a docked or floating card's header is a drawer's, with no room for a form to drop from); elsewhere feedback is `/feedback`, which takes words and no reaction. The console's Reminders button has no twin at the desk: the reminders page is the sidebar's menu's, as it was.
- What a space's chat held — its card up or not, its draft, its staged files — is kept for this run of the app only (its place across launches). A space's unseen answer (`answered`) is not kept across a quit either.
- A reminder's task shows nowhere by itself while it works: its notification, the past chats (*Reminder*, *Working*), and the reminders page say it. Two agents at once use the model twice over; nothing bounds how many spaces work at a time.
