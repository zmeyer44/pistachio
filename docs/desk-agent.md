# The desk's agent

(Until 2026-10-09 the UI called a space a "tab group", and a Profile a "Space"; identifiers keep the old words.)

A space is a task, shown as its desk (`docs/spaces.md`), and the agent can work at it with the person. The desk is the agent's context and its bounds: it sees every window and the space's tabs not out, keeps the task's own files and facts, and works in the space's tabs, where the person can watch it. Concepts and the choice between them: the "Desk Companion" study (claude.ai artifact, 30 Sep 2026). Chosen: the **Co-worker**, in the **Bar**, with the context as a **Stack**, the last row under the space's tabs in the sidebar (it was in the desk's own dock until the sidebar became the dock, 1 Oct 2026); the agent may rearrange windows; one conversation per space, any conversation can be continued in a space; the space's context syncs.

Desktop only (the desk is). Builds on `docs/desk.md` (the desk itself), `docs/console-routing.md` (turns) and `docs/notes.md` (the sync pattern the context follows).

## 1. What a person does

**The Bar** (2026-10-08, the "Droplet": `DeskBar.tsx`, `DeskNub.tsx`, `nub-motion.ts`). A nub in the desk's trailing foot corner: a droplet of the shell's own ground — a 36px circle partly sunk into the corner (its centre 20px in from both edges), joined to the foot and the trailing edge by 11px fillets, as if about to fall from the corner. It is not painted: it is a hole through the well and the windows under it, so it is the ground whatever the theme (docs/desk.md, *The foot*); over a live page main's notch view draws it. Its face is Pistachio's own mark, the nut of the brand mark (`PistachioGlyph`), in the space's colour (the agent's sparkle until 2026-10-08); while the agent works a thin arc of that colour runs round it, and while the answer floats a green dot sits on it. The pointer resting on it (80ms, so one passing over does not) swells it, the circle growing and drawn out of the corner as if pulled from it — 22px round, its centre 27px in, standing clear of both edges — while its fillets tighten (11 → 6px), so where it meets the edges reaches no further along them (the user's call, 2026-10-08: the circle grows, not the joins; it was 21px, 23px in, its fillets unchanged and its footprint along the edges widening) (the plus → menu morph's open, 350ms with a little overshoot; back 250ms, calm), and no page gives way for that (over a live page main's notch view draws the swell, read off the same clock as the shell's); resting there, its tooltip names the space and ⌘I — over a live page too, the view's hover opening it as the shell's nub's own does (`viewHover`), the band it appears in a cover like every Bar tooltip's, so the page under it is a still while it is up. A click (or Enter) lets out its **menu** (2026-10-08, the user's call: the menu's close a button of its own, its droplets as round as it and fanned out rather than in a column): the nub fills with the card's fill (its outline painted over its hole) and lets go of the desk's edges — what is of it in the corner, its hole and the fill over it, drains into the corner as it lifts out, pulling a neck of goo from there that snaps, and it stands free, a 40px circle 8px clear of the foot and the trailing edge (`NUB_FREE`, `nubLetGo`), its mark the menu's close — and three droplets as round as it fan out of it over the desk's quarter, 70px from its centre (`NUB_FAN_R`): the prompt along the foot (180°), the microphone between (135°), past chats up the trailing edge (90°), each on a spring with a small overshoot, swept round from the foot up (28ms apart), stretched with its speed and pulling a neck of goo from the nub that thins and snaps; closing runs them back into it, the last out first back, as it settles into the corner again, and the fill goes once it is in. The goo is the public technique: the shapes blurred (5px) and their alpha thresholded (`feColorMatrix … 0 0 0 20 -8.5`) in one SVG, the necks lines drawn from the nub to the droplets, the buttons crisp above it; the whole layer is clipped to the desk's corner so neither the fill nor its shadow reaches the gutter. Each droplet has a tooltip to its left (the prompt's with ⌘I); the arrows move between them, Escape and a press anywhere else (a live page's too) put them back, the nub's own click too; the nub's mark turns into a close while they are out. A capture slows all of it (`--desk-nub-time-scale`).

The prompt's droplet becomes the **pill**: it detaches and morphs (its glass from the droplet's box to the pill's, a little past and back; its row fading and blurring in as it is uncovered) into a floating glass field at the desk's foot, 52px tall — the Bar's own height before the nub, its row's padding, buttons (30px, send 32px) and field as they were then (the user's call, 2026-10-08, after a day at 40px) — and up to 480px wide, its words 15px, right-aligned 54px in from the trailing edge so the nub stands beside it — tied to the nub by a thin bridge of goo for the first half of the morph, then let go (the goo layer lies under the pill's glass, so only the bridge shows). It holds the message field (its placeholder names the space), a tray (attach, a new conversation), the answer chevron, the microphone and send (stop while the agent works; the field then steers), each button with a tooltip (drawn under a cover, `bar-tip`). It stays out while anything holds it — something typed, staged or warned of, dictation, the agent at work, the answer docked on it, the @ list, files on their way, the keyboard in it — and goes back into the nub (shrinking into its face, calm) as soon as none does; Escape with nothing in it puts it back. While it is out, its **close** stands above the nub (2026-10-08, the user's call): a small round button, 32px, 6px in from the trailing edge where the menu's droplets once stood in a column (`nubClose`), the card's fill and a droplet's shadow, coming up out of the nub as the pill comes out and going back into it (not while the menu is out, where it would stand among the droplets). It puts the pill away whatever holds it — what was typed and staged is kept for when it comes out again, a recording is dropped, and the answer docked on it closes (a floating one stays) — which a press elsewhere does not do to a pill that holds something. Put away so (`stowed`), the pill stays away until something asks for it again: the keyboard coming to its field (⌘I, a droplet), files dragged to it, an answer docking on it. The pill and the nub's menu are one or the other, never both (the user's call, 2026-10-08: the menu's droplets fanned across the pill's end): the nub pressed with the pill out puts the pill away as its close does — what is in it kept, the answer docked on it closed — and lets out the menu; whatever asks for the pill puts the menu back in. ⌘I puts the keyboard in its field (on the desktop, ⌘I is the Bar's, not the sidebar chat's — also from inside a page, which main relays), the pill then growing out of the nub itself. Its box is always laid out where it rests (the answer docks above it, a floating one keeps clear of it). Everything out of the nub is a cover (`bar`, the nub's box with its fan; `pill`; `pill-close`; `conversations`), drawn once the pages under it have given way (or after 300ms), and a cover until it is back in (280ms). Escape unwinds one layer at a time: the conversations, the menu, the answer docked, the pill if empty. Until 2026-10-08 the Bar was a notch centred on the desk's foot that grew into a 52px field there; until 2026-10-02 a glass pill floating a gap above the desk's foot, the desk keeping a band for it (`BAR_BAND`).

**Dictation.** The nub's microphone droplet opens the pill already listening, and the pill's microphone button listens: the field gives way to a waveform of what it hears, scrolling in from the trailing end, and a clock, with discard (×) at the leading end and Done (✓) where send was. Done, or Enter, transcribes what was said with the host's speech-to-text (main's `transcribeSpeech`, the walkthrough's) and puts it in the field where the caret was, spaced from the words either side, to be read back and sent; it is never sent by itself. Escape or × drops the recording, even one being transcribed. A tap under 0.8 s is not sent; a recording stops itself at two minutes and is transcribed. A refused microphone, a failed transcription or silence says so in the Bar's banner, and the typed text stays. The logic is `lib/dictation.ts` (`Dictation`, free of React, and `useDictation`); the waveform is `components/desk/DictationWave.tsx`.

**The answer.** A card of the same glass, resting on the pill, as wide as it (they share one column; 2026-10-08, the user's call — it was 440px, a little narrower), however tall the pill grows, opening out of the pill's trailing end (its clip grows from a circle there, the plus → menu morph's open, played once as it comes: a clip left on it would make it a backdrop root, its glass blurring nothing), shows the latest exchange: what was asked, the agent's steps as it takes them, its reply as it streams, and anything it asks (a question, an approval, a takeover). It opens when a turn starts (the person's message, or the agent asking something) and stays until collapsed (its chevron, or Escape); the Bar's chevron brings it back. "Whole conversation" in its header shows every turn, scrolled to the end. While the card is up, the windows under it are stills (it is a cover). No "I'm on it" intro and no "browsing in another tab" chip at a desk: the agent is seen on the windows.

**The answer, detached** (2026-10-07; `components/desk/answer-motion.ts`, `lib/desk/answer-float.ts`). The answer's header is what it is taken by. Pressed, the card lifts (a hair larger, its shadow deeper, a sheen); pulled, it follows the pointer 1:1, still looking docked, and past 42px it tears off into a window: its frame morphs to a window's size (480 wide, the pill's at its widest, or the size it was last left at) and corners (18 → 14px) under the hand, the content laid out once at the new size and clipped to the frame as it goes, a still copy of how it was fading out over it; its header gains a grip and a dock button in place of the chevron. Let go before 42px, it springs back into its place. Floating, it is a window: a press elsewhere (a live page's too) and Escape leave it be. It is moved by its header, and thrown as the desk's windows are (Glide, a rebound that settles flush), settling into the nearest of six anchors (each side's top, middle and foot) when thrown toward one or let go near one, and flush with an edge it is let go near; it rests clear of the Bar. Its edges and corners resize it (the ring straddles its border, mostly outside it; no smaller than 320×220), the opposite edge holding still. The pill's chevron becomes a floating-window mark with a green dot (the nub wears the dot too), and pressing it calls the card out (a ring flashes round it) rather than hiding it; the pill itself goes back into the nub, as with no card up. Docked, its slot is right-aligned on the pill (`dockSlot`, `DOCK_W`), and the zone that takes it back is above the pill. Brought back over the Bar, a ghost of the docked slot comes up as it approaches (never as it leaves: a card just torn off must leave the zone once before it counts), brightening once the card's foot is down at the slot (or the pointer on the Bar), the card shrinking a little and leaning toward it; let go there — or thrown down into it, or its dock button, or a double press of its header — and it morphs back into the slot, the ghost dissolving into it. Held over the Bar it goes no lower than its slot (it sits on the Bar, never slides under it). Escape mid-gesture puts it back where the gesture began (a tear goes back onto the Bar). Whether it floats, where (a share of the lane) and its size are kept on this device (`pistachio.desk.answer.v1`), so the next answer opens where the last was left. Its motion is imperative: one animation frame loop writes its transforms and layout size (React draws its content, never its place); the drag layer holds the pointer over live pages (`startPaneDrag`); its cover follows it as drawn, reaching 48px ahead while it moves so the pages it heads over give way first, and it stays shown while they do. The windows under a floating card are stills while it lies over them, as under the docked one — but floating, it is a window among the desk's (`DeskEngine.stackCover`): a window the person brings up (a press, the dock, the sidebar) comes in front of it, its page live again, and the card shows where none is over it; a press on the card, a turn starting (the person's message, the agent asking), or the Bar's button brings it back in front of them all. A press on a window also takes the keyboard from the Bar, whose grown cover would otherwise keep that page a still. Reduced motion: no lift, overshoot or cross-fade — it moves and changes size at once.

**The agent at the desk.**
- *Where it is*: the window whose tab it is working in (`agentDrivenTabId`) wears the agent's comet ring on its frame and a chip saying what it is doing (Reading, Clicking, Typing, Opening, Arranging, Noting, Thinking between steps) — in the bar frame's title row, or above a tab or handle frame. The tab's row in the sidebar wears the ring too (on its icon). The page itself glows while the agent drives it (the glow main already injects).
- *Windows it opens* join the space and come out onto the desk beside the window in use, under it in the stack, never taking the keyboard or the selection.
- *Arranging*: it may place, tile, cascade, put away and bring out windows. After a turn that moved windows, the answer card offers **Undo layout**, which puts every window back where it was before the turn.
- *Notes on windows*: it may pin a short note (80 characters) to a window's frame ("Lands 11:05, before check-in"), beside the chip. Notes stay until the next turn starts or the person dismisses them (×). A note needs the window out on the desk.
- *Out of the space*: moving a tab out of the space is asked first.

**Conversations.** Each space has its own. The desk's conversation is the current space's (or a new, empty one), and passing the desk to another space opens that space's; main follows the current space itself (`runs.followGroup`). (Until 2026-10-09 opening the desk opened the space's conversation, and leaving it went back to the conversation that was open before.) The nub's past-chats droplet itself grows into the list (the plus → menu morph: its glass grows up and left from where the droplet rests, its corners relaxing from the droplet's round into 14px, its mark cross-fading out as the rows slide in; back into the droplet quicker and calm, or fading as it shrinks when the menu goes too; while it is out the other droplets run back into the nub, out of its way, and fan out again when it goes back with the menu still out), 320px wide with thin rows (30px); it lists every conversation, each marked with where it started (This space, another space by name, Another space when that space is not here, Sidebar, Reminder): choosing one continues it here, and from then on it is this space's (the space it came from keeps it too). New conversation, the list's first row, starts an empty one for the space. A press anywhere else (a live page's too, which main relays) puts the list and the menu away; Escape puts the list back into its droplet, and a second the menu into the nub; choosing a conversation puts both away. While the agent is acting, the console is its: nothing can be chosen, and the session follows the current space again once the turn ends.

**The Stack.** The space's context: the *Context* row under the space's tabs in the sidebar, a pile of sheets with its count (empty, one dashed sheet and *Drop files*; on the rail, the pile alone with the count on it). Drop files from Finder on it (pictures, PDFs, text, markdown, CSV, calendar, JSON, Word and Excel files), or text and links dragged out of a page; it bounces when something comes in. Click it to open its card beside the sidebar, over the desk: the files as tiles, the facts, snippets and links as lines, **Add files…**, a field to type a fact, and × on each. A file opens on the desk as a document window, out of the Stack, and goes back into it when collapsed (`docs/desk-documents.md`); a dot marks one whose window is out, and ↗ opens it in its own app (a private temporary copy). Files dropped on the desk itself join the Stack too, and open where they were let go; in the Bar, `@` mentions one, which then rides with the message. What the agent saved is marked (✦, "Pistachio ·"). Contexts of spaces not in this Profile's list — another Mac's, synced, or another Profile's — are offered under **From another Mac or Profile**, to bring into this space (a copy).

## 2. What the agent gets

**Scope.** A turn in the desk's conversation runs on a scoped browser: `tabs_list` lists only the space's tabs and the agent's own hidden ones, and every page tool refuses any other tab with an error that says to ask the person and open its address with `tab_open`. `tab_open` opens a hidden tab, as everywhere on the desktop (`BrowserController.openHiddenTab`): out of the space, off the desk, out of the person's sight, so the agent looks things up while the desk stays as the person has it. A page the agent's own click or key opens is hidden too (`holdPopupsHidden`). Only `tab_show` puts a hidden tab on the desk — when the person asks to open or see a page, or to put it on the desk: it joins the space (`showHiddenTabInGroup`) and comes out quietly, beside the window in use. The scope is the controller's, not the prompt's: `DeskScope` wraps `DesktopBrowserBackend`. (A page's own `window.open` or `target="_blank"` from one of the desk's tabs on the person's click opens beside it in its space, before it is shown: `BrowserController`'s window-open handler, `#onDesk`, `createTab({ beside })`. A desk turn holds its space for as long as it runs (`holdGroup`), so this goes on after the person passes the desk to another space mid-turn.)

**The desk block.** Each turn's message carries the desk as it stands, read from the shell at the turn's start: the space's name, every window (tab id, title, address, box as percent of the desk, stacking, which is in use), the space's tabs not out, and the context's items (id, kind, name or text). A few hundred tokens; pages are read with the page tools as ever.

**Desk tools** (tool group `desk`, host-supplied, only on a desk turn):

| Tool | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `desk_state` | The desk block, fresh. |
| `desk_arrange` | Tile or cascade every window; place windows in a zone (halves, quarters, center, full) or a box; put windows away into the sidebar; bring the space's other tabs out. Focus and the keyboard never move. |
| `desk_note` | Pin a short note to a window's frame, or clear it. |
| `desk_ungroup` | Move tabs out of the space (they stay open). Asked of the person first. |
| `context_read` | Read a context item: a fact's text, a text file's text, an image or PDF as a file the model sees. |
| `context_save` | Save a fact or snippet to the space's context. |

Desk turns always take the browser path (no answer-path routing, route basis `skipped`): the desk tools and pages are the point of a desk. `DESK_RULES` join the browse prompt: work from the desk block, stay on the desk, arrange confidently (Undo exists) and say so, note sparingly, read and save context, ask before ungrouping, name windows by title.

## 3. How it works

**Desk session** (`RunController`). The session follows the current space, which is main's own (`BrowserControllerHooks.onCurrentGroupChange` → `runs.followGroup`, `docs/spaces.md` §2); the shell sends only `choose` and `new` (`deskConversation`), each checked against `currentGroupId()`. While a session is up: the open thread is the space's (bound in `desk-conversations.json`, space → thread, `DeskConversationStore`), every turn in it is desk-scoped, a thread started in it is marked with the space (`RunSummary.groupId`, on `ThreadListItem` too) and bound to the space whose Bar it was. A turn is desk-scoped when its space is bound to the open thread (never a cloud run). A turn keeps the space it started in (`holdGroup`); when it ends the session follows the current space again. Main keeps one open thread, as always; the desk only chooses which. It works on an empty space too (no tab in use). (Until 2026-10-09 the shell said when a desk was up and for which space — `deskConversation` `enter`, again for each space it passed to — and when it was left, `leave`, which went back to the conversation open before; a shell reload left the desk, `did-start-loading`. `enter` and `leave` are kept as no-ops so an older shell's command is not refused.)

**Desk bridge** (`desk-bridge.ts`). Main asks the shell for what only the shell knows (the desk's layout) and to change it: a request with an id on `pistachio:desk-request`, the reply by `deskReply(id, …)`, with a 5 s timeout (a failed reply is an ordinary tool error). Every request names the space whose desk it is for, and the shell refuses one for a desk no longer in view before it changes anything (the person may pass the desk to another space mid-turn). The shell answers from the desk's engine (`desk-requests.ts`, fed by `DeskSurface`), trimming titles and addresses to the reply's bounds (`MAX_DESK_TITLE`, `MAX_DESK_URL`, `MAX_DESK_DOCKED`): `state` (`agentLayout`), `arrange` (`arrangeFor`, which refuses strangers, putting away the window in use, or a person's gesture in progress, and then moves nothing), `bringOut` (waits up to 2 s for the tab to reach the shell's space, then `bringOutQuietly` — under the window in use, no selection), `note`. Each reply is checked in main to be the same space's desk. Before the first change of a turn the shell keeps `layoutSnapshot()`; the answer card's **Undo layout** is `restoreLayout` for that turn. A window still on its way into the sidebar when it is wanted back (Undo, or the agent placing or bringing it out again) turns back (`#recall`, making room first on a full desk) rather than being lost to the dock; one on its way into the Close pad or another space's icon goes on — that was the person's doing.

**The Bar's notch** (`DeskEngine.setNotch`, `setNotchShape`). The idle notch's size, centred on the desk's foot, is what windows under it are cut short of; the notch as drawn now is the hole cut through the well and the windows. The engine's `#usable` and `#reach` are the whole card (until 2026-10-02 they stopped above the Bar's band, `setBarBand`). The agent's zones and boxes are percents of `#usable` (`lib/desk/agent.ts`: `deskZoneRect`, `percentBox`, `boxRect`).

**A space's context** (`group-context-store.ts`). One index file and content-addressed files in user data (`group-context.json`, `group-blobs/<sha24>`, owner-only permissions). Synced as two record kinds, sealed with the account's workspace key like notes: `group-context:<groupId>` (LWW: the space's title when written, its items, and the files' ids) and `group-blob:<sha24>` (a file's bytes, only files up to `MAX_GROUP_BLOB_BYTES`; bigger ones stay on the Mac they were dropped on and are listed elsewhere as not here). Spaces do not sync live between Macs; a space pulled from another Mac keeps its id, so its context is there with it.

## 5. Not done / open questions

- Masking a region of a page (`window_mask`), pointing at an element in a page, and closing tabs are not desk tools yet.
- Cloud runs have no desk: a cloud conversation opened in a space is not desk-scoped.
- The context's files are read by the model as they are (a PDF as a PDF); nothing is summarised when it is dropped.
- A context brought from another Mac is a copy: the two spaces' contexts go their own ways afterwards.
- The Stack's files show as type glyphs, not previews.
- A text or link dragged out of a live page onto the Stack is untested against real pages (the e2e drops a file synthetically).
- No live-model measurement yet of how well the agent uses the desk tools (the e2e and unit tests script the model).
- The cloud browser's record allow-list does not carry space contexts (by design: it has no desk).
- Dictation is transcribed when the person is done, not word by word as they speak (the host's speech-to-text takes a whole recording). The sidebar console's composer has its own copy of the same recording logic; it could move onto `useDictation`.
