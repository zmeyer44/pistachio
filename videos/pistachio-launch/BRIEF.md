## Intent

A launch video for Pistachio (pistachio.run), "a browser built for tomorrow." Sell, not tour.
User's words: "exciting, engaging, modern, and worthy of a contemporary product launch. Its UI,
colors, typography, branding, design language, and motion language should feel unmistakably
Pistachio." All creative choices (concept, story, copy, pacing, imagery, animation) delegated.

## Customizations

- No narration: no TTS provider available (HeyGen signed out, Kokoro not installed). Kinetic on-screen copy carries the story.
- Music bed synthesized locally at a fixed tempo so cuts land on the beat; UI SFX from the bundled library.
- Product UI rebuilt in HTML from the site's own shell design (crisp at 1080p); real site assets: wallpaper, logo mark, feature screenshots.

## Notes

- Source of truth for claims: pistachio.run copy. Keep claims accurate: Mac (Apple silicon), early preview, agent works in signed-in sessions, lists each step, typing interrupts it, open source (GPL-3.0), local by default.
- Don't disparage competitors by name.
